Fix cross-device call sync + real OS call popup with Answer/Decline

Declining/accepting on one owner device never reached other devices
(phone/PC) because accept/decline/end/expire were only broadcast to the
call's own signaling room, which non-participating devices never join
until they accept. Now also broadcast to the owner's device room, and
have the frontend subscribe to that directly instead of relying solely
on a poll that was paused the whole time a call was ringing.

Also give incoming calls a real OS notification (not just the in-app
ringtone) whenever the tab isn't actually visible - including a
Decline action that hits a new public /api/calls/:roomId/decline
endpoint straight from the service worker, so it works even with the
app fully closed.
